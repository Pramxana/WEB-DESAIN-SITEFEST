Learn the basic controls and mission requirements for each virtual laboratory.
Introduction & Basic Controls

Eksperika is an interactive virtual science laboratory that helps students learn science through visual simulations, guided missions, and experiment feedback. Use the sidebar to navigate between different scientific disciplines.

  • Start Experiment: Open the selected lab or begin an activity from the dashboard.
  • Reset: Return the simulation to its initial state to try again.
  • Challenge: Start a mission-based learning task and earn scores.
Chemistry Tutorial

In Chemistry Lab, users can mix virtual substances and observe how the reaction changes the liquid color, status, and equation safely without using real laboratory materials.

  • 1
    Choose a chemical tube from the available options.
  • 2
    Add or select the material into the pending mix queue.
  • 3
    Press React & Mix to execute the chemical combination.
  • 4
    Observe the liquid color, bubbles, temperature changes, reaction status, and equation in the analysis panel.
Physics Tutorial

Physics Lab focuses on electrical current flows and electronic parts behavior based on Ohm's law.

  • 1
    Choose a circuit type: series, parallel, or mixed.
  • 2
    Add components such as battery, bulb, resistor, switch, voltmeter, or ammeter to the grid.
  • 3
    Adjust the voltage or resistance using the provided sliders.
  • 4
    Observe the current measurement, lamp brightness, oscilloscope waveform, and circuit behavior.
Biology Tutorial

Explore human body systems interactively with detailed observation data.

  • 1
    Select a body part, organ, or physiological system from the view.
  • 2
    Activate the Scan Tool to enter diagnosis/observation mode.
  • 3
    Read the information panel regarding vitals and system interactions.
  • 4
    Complete the observation or diagnosis tasks based on provided clinical data.
Challenge Mode Rules

Test your knowledge in constrained environments. Validation strictly follows these sequences:

  • 1
    You must press Start Challenge first. The timer and mission validation will not work otherwise.
  • 2
    Read the mission objective displayed on the screen.
  • 3
    Complete the required interactions (mixing, circuit building, or scanning) before the timer runs out.
  • 4
    Submit or activate the result button.
  • 5
    Read the Mission Complete or Mission Failed feedback popup to understand your score.
